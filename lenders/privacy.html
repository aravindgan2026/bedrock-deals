Privacy

Here is, in plain words, what we do with what you send us.

What we collect

Your name, company, email and phone. The details of each deal you send. Any papers you upload.

Why we collect it

To look at your deal and set terms. Nothing more.

Who sees it

You, and Bedrock staff who work on deals. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

While we work on the deal, and after that for our records.

How to ask us to delete it

Email info@mybedrock.app and tell us what to remove. We will answer you.

Uploads

Only upload papers you have permission to share. If a paper has someone else's private details, make sure the sender said it is okay.
